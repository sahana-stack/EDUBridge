The Core Problem

Student progress shouldn't live in disconnected places.

When academic marks, home habits, and classroom observations exist in separate silos, parents and teachers miss the full picture.

Parent View

Teacher View

Tracks test evaluation scores, attendance, and classroom participation, but doesn't see home routine challenges.
